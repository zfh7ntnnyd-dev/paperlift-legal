What Paperlift is

Paperlift is a local flying game for iPhone. You fly a paper plane, earn XP and level up, unlock planes (folds) and contrails, and chase scores and achievements. You do not create an account with us.

Optional features:

  • Game Center (Apple) for scores and achievements
  • Rewarded video ads (Google AdMob) you choose to watch — Continue Flying +1UP (the “Continue + 1UP” button: keep flying after a crash) and Double XP (the “Double it” button: double the XP from that run)
  • Soft ads (Google AdMob) — a banner on the death / results card, and an interstitial after some Fly again taps (not mid-flight, not on the title or Main Menu)
  • Local notifications (optional) — a daily sky reminder, a Mystery Box reminder, and a notice when you lose a top-3 spot on today’s leaderboard. They are scheduled on your device.

This shipped version offers no in-app purchases (no Remove Ads product and no Paper packs). The app only checks for an earlier Gold Leaf purchase so it can restore that plane.

Data we collect / process

We do not operate user accounts and we do not maintain a server-side profile of players.

1. Data that stays on your device (local only)

Stored with Apple’s UserDefaults on your device, for gameplay:

  • High scores and daily best
  • XP and level
  • Paper balance (from earlier versions)
  • Planes (folds) and contrails
  • Daily streak
  • Achievement progress
  • Mystery Box progress
  • Your recent position on today’s Game Center leaderboard (cached for the title screen)
  • Small app settings, such as whether you were already asked about reminders

Paperlift does not send these saves to us or to ad companies. If you use Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple (section 3). The ad and measurement SDKs in sections 4 and 5 collect their own data, described there. Deleting the app deletes this local data (subject to any iCloud / device backups you manage yourself).

Privacy Manifest: the app declares UserDefaults access under Apple’s required-reason API category (CA92.1).

2. Analytics and measurement

Paperlift’s own gameplay logs (for example, a run starting or ending) are written on-device with Apple’s os_log. Paperlift does not send them anywhere.

The app does include two SDKs that send data off the device. Google AdMob measures the ads it serves (section 4). Meta’s Facebook SDK measures installs and app opens for Paperlift’s Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns (section 5). Both are declared in the app’s Privacy Manifest (section 6), including Product Interaction data used for analytics.

3. Apple — Game Center (optional)

If you sign in to Game Center, scores and achievements may be sent to Apple. That processing is governed by Apple’s privacy policy and Game Center terms. You can decline Game Center and still play.

5. Meta (Facebook SDK) — install measurement

Paperlift includes Meta’s Facebook SDK (FacebookCore) to measure its install ads on Facebook and Instagram. Paperlift does not show Meta ads, and there is no Facebook login.

  • App events: the SDK automatically logs app installs and app opens, and Paperlift logs one “Completed tutorial” event on your first launch.
  • Apple SKAdNetwork and Meta Aggregated Event Measurement: used to measure installs without the advertising identifier. If you open Paperlift from a Facebook or Instagram ad link, that link is passed to Meta’s SDK.
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA): collection is off by default. It is turned on only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt and, where Google’s consent rules apply, your consent choices allow ads.
  • Limited Data Use: where consent is required and you have not given it, Paperlift also turns on Meta’s Limited Data Use option.

Meta may process additional information it receives with these events (for example device and network information), as described in Meta’s policies. We do not sell your personal information.

Meta privacy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On supported versions of iOS, Paperlift may request permission via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt so AdMob can show more relevant / personalized ads and so Meta’s SDK can use the advertising identifier for install measurement. The in-app usage string is:

Paperlift uses this to show you more relevant ads.

The ATT prompt is requested around the first death / results card (not on launch or the title screen).

  • You can tap Don’t Allow and still play. Optional rewarded ads can still work; they may be less personalized. Soft ads may still appear. Meta’s SDK does not collect the advertising identifier; install measurement uses SKAdNetwork and Aggregated Event Measurement instead.
  • Change later: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • If Allow Apps to Request to Track is off system-wide, iOS will not show the prompt (treated as denied).

Consent / UMP (where required)

Where required (for example in regions covered by Google’s consent requirements such as the EEA/UK), Paperlift uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) so you can make ad privacy choices. The consent form, when required, is shown on the title screen. When privacy options are required, the in-app Privacy screen (on the Edit plane screen) shows Ad privacy choices so you can change them. Your consent choices also limit Meta’s SDK as described in section 5.

Paperlift is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app includes AdMob and Meta’s SDK and declares tracking for third-party advertising, it is not intended for use by children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information through the app in a way that concerns you, contact us at the email above.

(We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 for our own account systems — we do not operate accounts.)

Retention

  • Local game data: retained on your device until you delete the app or clear app data.
  • Game Center: retained by Apple under Apple’s policies.
  • AdMob / Google: retained by Google under Google’s policies.
  • Meta: retained by Meta under Meta’s policies.
  • We do not operate a Paperlift server or cloud database of player data.

Sharing

We share or enable sharing of data only as described above:

  • Apple (Game Center, if you use it; and Apple platform services generally)
  • Google (AdMob for rewarded and soft ads)
  • Meta (Facebook SDK for install and app-open measurement)

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share local UserDefaults game saves with other companies for their independent marketing.

We rely on Apple’s on-device storage for local saves and on Apple’s, Google’s and Meta’s security practices for their services. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. Please use device passcodes / biometrics and keep iOS updated.
