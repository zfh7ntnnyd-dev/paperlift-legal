What Paperlift is

Paperlift is a local flying game for iPhone. You fly a paper plane, earn Paper currency, unlock folds, and chase scores and achievements. You do not create an account with us.

Optional features:

  • Game Center (Apple) for scores and achievements
  • Rewarded video ads (Google AdMob) you choose to watch — Keep flying (revive after a crash) and Double Paper
  • Soft ads (Google AdMob) — a banner on the death / results card, and an interstitial after some Fly again taps (not mid-flight, not on the title or Main Menu)
  • Local notifications (optional daily sky reminder)

This shipped version has no in-app purchases (no Remove Ads product and no Paper packs).

Data we collect / process

We do not operate user accounts and we do not maintain a server-side profile of players.

1. Data that stays on your device (local only)

Stored with Apple’s UserDefaults on your device, for gameplay:

  • High scores
  • Paper currency
  • Folds
  • Daily streak
  • Achievement progress

This local save data does not leave the app unless you use Game Center or interact with ads (see below). Deleting the app deletes this local data (subject to any iCloud / device backups you manage yourself).

Privacy Manifest: the app declares UserDefaults access under Apple’s required-reason API category (CA92.1).

2. Analytics (on-device only)

Gameplay/analytics events are logged on-device via Apple’s os_log only. There is no remote analytics vendor in this shipped version. Nothing from that sink is sent to a third-party analytics service by Paperlift.

3. Apple — Game Center (optional)

If you sign in to Game Center, scores and achievements may be sent to Apple. That processing is governed by Apple’s privacy policy and Game Center terms. You can decline Game Center and still play.

App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On supported versions of iOS, Paperlift may request permission via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt so AdMob can show more relevant / personalized ads. The in-app usage string is:

Paperlift uses this to show you more relevant ads.

The ATT prompt is requested around the first death / results card (not on launch or the title screen).

  • You can tap Don’t Allow and still play. Optional rewarded ads can still work; they may be less personalized. Soft ads may still appear.
  • Change later: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • If Allow Apps to Request to Track is off system-wide, iOS will not show the prompt (treated as denied).

Consent / UMP (where required)

Where required (for example in regions covered by Google’s consent requirements such as the EEA/UK), Paperlift uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) so you can make ad privacy choices. When privacy options are required, the in-app Privacy screen (under Folds / Store) can present Ad privacy choices.

Children / age

Paperlift is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app includes AdMob and declares tracking for third-party advertising, it is not intended for use by children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information through the app in a way that concerns you, contact us at the email above.

(We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 for our own account systems — we do not operate accounts.)

Retention

  • Local game data: retained on your device until you delete the app or clear app data.
  • Game Center: retained by Apple under Apple’s policies.
  • AdMob / Google: retained by Google under Google’s policies.
  • We do not operate a separate Paperlift cloud database of player profiles.

Sharing

We share or enable sharing of data only as described above:

  • Apple (Game Center, if you use it; and Apple platform services generally)
  • Google (AdMob for rewarded and soft ads)

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share local UserDefaults game saves with other companies for their independent marketing.

Security

We rely on Apple’s on-device storage for local saves and on Apple’s and Google’s security practices for their services. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. Please use device passcodes / biometrics and keep iOS updated.
